# Cinematic Video Scenes

Pre-written scene descriptions for FAL Seedance / video generation pipelines. Each block is a concise English description that feeds into KimiCliDirect to be refined into a full video prompt.

---

### Template A: Morning Tea Ritual

```
A young East Asian woman performs a quiet morning tea ceremony in a traditional wooden room. Soft dawn light filters through rice-paper screens. She moves with deliberate grace — warming the teapot, pouring steam rising into a ceramic cup. The camera slowly orbits around her at waist height. Contemplative, peaceful, warm amber tones, dust motes in light beams. 35mm film grain.
```

### Template B: Rainy Bamboo Walk

```
The same woman walks alone on a wet stone path through a misty bamboo forest. She holds a dark oil-paper umbrella. Rain drips from bamboo leaves. Cool blue-gray overcast light, shallow depth of field, her white skirt hem catching droplets. The camera tracks alongside her at a respectful distance. Melancholic, serene, sound of rain.
```

### Template C: Window Contemplation

```
Interior evening scene. The woman sits by a rain-streaked lattice window, holding a warm ceramic cup with both hands. City lights blur in the wet glass behind her. A single brass table lamp casts a honey-gold glow on her face. She gazes outward, lost in thought. Camera slowly pushes in. Intimate, lonely, beautiful.
```

### Template D: Skincare Ritual (BOTANIKA Close-up)

```
A quiet skincare ritual in a warm bathroom. The woman opens an amber BOTANIKA moisturizer bottle and presses the pump. She applies the cream to her face with slow, mindful strokes. Soft mirror reflection visible. Warm tungsten lighting, steam from a nearby bath. The camera holds on her hands and the bottle, then gently pushes in. Sensory, intimate, self-care.
```

### Template E: Evening Wind-down

```
Night scene in a cozy reading nook. The woman sits in a worn leather armchair beside a tall bookshelf, reading by the light of a single candle. A wool blanket drapes over her lap. Rain still falls outside. Camera slowly orbits from behind the chair to a profile view. Peaceful, solitary, deeply calm. Warm amber and deep shadow.
```

### Template F: Garden Stillness

```
Twilight in a Japanese garden. The woman kneels beside a moss-covered stone basin, washing her hands in cold spring water. Fireflies begin to appear in the deepening blue air. A stone lantern glows softly. Camera holds wide, then slowly pushes in. Ethereal, timeless, connection with nature.
```

### Template G: City Night Walk

```
The woman walks through neon-lit Tokyo backstreets at night. Reflections of pink and blue neon shimmer on wet asphalt. She wears the same black blouse and white skirt, now windswept. She pauses before a vending machine, its glow illuminating her face. Cinematic cyber-noir meets traditional elegance. Camera tracks from behind.
```

### Template H: Letter Writing

```
A quiet study room. The woman sits at a wooden desk writing with a fountain pen by candlelight. Ink bottle, pressed flowers, and old letters scattered on the desk. She pauses, looks out the rain-streaked window, then continues writing. Camera slowly pushes in over her shoulder to the paper. Nostalgic, intimate, timeless.
```

### Template I: Onsen Reflection

```
Early morning outdoor onsen. The woman relaxes in steaming hot spring water, eyes closed, head resting against smooth river rocks. Mist rises around her. Snow-capped mountains visible in the distance. She opens her eyes and looks toward the horizon. Camera holds wide, then gentle push-in. Pure tranquility, release, warmth against cold air.
```
