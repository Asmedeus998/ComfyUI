## Template Filling Guide

1. **Replace all `[BRACKETED]` placeholders** with concise, vivid descriptions. Keep each segment under ~250 words total.
2. **Camera movements:** Use exactly one per time slice (e.g., "slow dolly in", "static wide", "gentle push", "orbiting medium shot").
3. **Audio tokens:** Keep `{audio: ...}` descriptions short — mood + instrumentation, e.g., `{audio: pulsing electronic bass with rising synth}`.
4. **@Image9 (continuation):** In Segments 2–4, if a real continuation frame is supplied, the segment MUST begin with `CONTINUE:` (NO timestamp prefix) describing the exact pose, hand position, gaze direction, and spatial coordinates from @Image9 (the continuation frame). Do not invent a new pose.
5. **No arc labels:** Do not write "Problem-Solution" or "Dramatic Reveal" inside the segment text; let the narrative beats imply the arc.
6. **No per-second timestamps:** Only use the four sparse ranges (0-3s, 3-7s, 7-11s, 11-15s) per segment.
7. **Reference hygiene:** Every segment must reference @Image1 through @Image8 at least once. @Image9 is only referenced in Segments 2–4.
8. **Semicolon discipline:** Use semicolons (`;`) to separate beats. Avoid periods inside the flowing paragraph sections.
